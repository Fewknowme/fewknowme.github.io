Privacy policy · RMDevelopers
Effective 19 October 2026

Your game stays on your device

Patang Pop is an offline puzzle game. It has no RMDevelopers account, game server, purchases or subscriptions. Levels and artwork are included in the app. Progress, best stars, active playing time, per-level best completion times, cosmetic thread, unlocked colours, the last daily-gift claim date, Kite Week event progress and settings are stored locally. We do not send this saved game content to our own server.

Optional cloud save with Google Play Games

From version 1.6.0, Patang Pop can back up your progress to your own Google account through Google Play Games Services “Saved Games”, so a new or reset phone gets it back. This is optional: the game is fully playable without it.

  • Sign-in. If you already use Google Play Games on your device, Play Games may sign you in automatically when the game starts; otherwise you can sign in from Settings → Cloud save. Google handles sign-in; we never see your Google password or email address.
  • What is saved. One save slot holding your thread balance and total thread earned, unlocked level, stars and best times, bonus and kite-colour unlocks, selected colour, hint credits, random puzzles cleared, the last daily-gift day and Kite Week progress, plus a short description and a progress number Google uses to pick the copy with more progress. Settings, reminder data, ad reward tokens and unfinished puzzle attempts stay on the device. No screenshots, contacts or location are saved.
  • Where it lives. The save is stored by Google in your Google account, not on an RMDevelopers server, and only this game can read or write it. Google Play Games also processes your Play Games player ID and profile (gamer name and avatar) and collects diagnostics and usage information about its SDK, as described in Google's Play Games Services data disclosure. Data is encrypted in transit.
  • When it syncs. When you open or return to the game and when you leave it. If this phone and your Google account both have different progress, the game asks which to keep before replacing anything.

Online advertising

The Android app includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. When network access and applicable privacy choices permit, Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and account identifiers including advertising ID and app set ID, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic/performance information. These are used for advertising, measurement/analytics, and fraud prevention. Google's SDK documentation states that this information is encrypted in transit. The game does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. Internet access is used for ads and, if you use it, cloud save.

All gameplay, hints, retries, level rewards and cosmetic unlocks are available without an internet connection. When a retry ad is available online, completing it starts a fresh attempt; closing early keeps the current attempt. Offline or unavailable retry ads allow a free retry. Optional rewarded videos provide extra cosmetic thread after completion, including a daily gift that can be claimed once per device calendar day. No account or login is required for this gift. Occasional interstitial ads may appear at level transitions when online. Bottom banner ads may appear on all game screens, including during play, when an ad is available. Banners sit separately below the game controls. Full-screen ads do not interrupt active puzzle moves.

See Google's privacy policy, Google's advertising information and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure for Google's practices.

Your choices and deletion

Where required, a Google consent message appears. Applicable ad privacy choices are available from Settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID using Android's privacy/ad settings. Denying personalised advertising may still permit limited or contextual ads where lawful; it does not restrict gameplay. You may play offline. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove local progress and settings. There is no RMDevelopers player account to delete. If you used cloud save, your saved game stays in your Google account after uninstalling; delete it, or your whole Play Games profile, in Google Play Games (your Play Games profile settings) or from your Google Account's data controls. Deleting it there also stops the game restoring that progress. Google's retention and deletion practices apply to data processed by its services; use Google's privacy controls or contact Google for those requests.

Audience and support

This general-audience logic game is intended for teenagers and adults, not specifically directed to children under 13. It does not ask players for their age, name or contact details. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to provide. We use it to respond to your request; do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. Support messages are retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.

For privacy or support requests, contact RMDevelopers at rohitmadasvpn@gmail.com. Include “Patang Pop” in the subject. This is also our contact for concerns about children's data.
