How a sky works

One rule. Five hundred knots.

Every kite points somewhere. Your job is to find the order that lets all of them out.

  1. 1

    Read the arrow

    Each kite flies in one direction. The dotted guide shows its path to the edge of the sky.

  2. 2

    Free the blocker

    Something in the way? Send that kite off first. A blocked tap costs one of your five tries.

  3. 3

    Clear the sky

    Empty the board to earn thread and up to three stars. Beat your own best time if you like. There is no timer.

The campaign

From three tutorials to a tangled butterfly.

Levels ramp up slowly, with easier relief boards between the hard ones. Expert skies have long chains of blockers and only a couple of safe first moves.

Questions

Before you download

Is Patang Pop free?

Yes. There are no purchases or subscriptions. The game is supported by ads.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every level, hint you have saved, and kite colour works without internet. Ads and cloud save need a connection, but the game never does.

How many levels are there?

500 campaign levels, including 492 shaped expert boards, plus a daily sky and endless random skies.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 or newer. Download size is small and there is no extra level download.

What does “patang” mean?

Patang is the Hindi word for kite. The game’s look comes from rooftop kite festivals like Makar Sankranti and Uttarayan.
